# Sources

Sources behind parameters and checks. Add one when a placeholder is sourced.

- Earth's atmospheric column: 101.3 kPa / 9.80665 m/s^2 = 10.3 t/m^2 (computed).
- Stanford torus (NASA SP-413, "Space Settlements: A Design Study", 1977): 10,000 people, a ring
  about 1.8 km across spun at 1 rpm. Used for scale; its exact figures are still to verify.
- Torus shell stress: meridional stress p r (2R - r) / (2 t (R - r)) at the inner equator
  (thin toroidal shell, Roark's Formulas for Stress and Strain).
- Power-limited rocket: Stuhlinger, "Ion Propulsion for Space Flight" (1964): characteristic
  velocity sqrt(2 alpha t).
